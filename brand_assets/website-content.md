## SECTION - How to use this pack

Copy under each page's SECTION labels is website wording. Page addresses, SEO fields, button destinations and anything marked SECTION - BUILD NOTE are instructions for the builder, not public copy.

This pack follows the latest decisions: no client accounts, no client portal, no instant checkout and no requirement for GoHighLevel or n8n to launch the brand website. The primary action is Request a Call: a visitor sends an enquiry, then WALKFLOW arranges the conversation manually.

Automation descriptions explain projects WALKFLOW can scope and develop. They must not be presented as ready-to-use software subscriptions. Demonstrations are concepts until their actual functionality has been tested. Do not advertise delivery availability before you are ready to accept that work.

Policy pages are working drafts for the proposed launch setup. Complete the marked business details and verify the actual data practices before publication. These website terms do not replace a signed client agreement.
